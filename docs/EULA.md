## Please know

- **No warranty.** Hexward is provided as is. No antivirus catches everything, and Hexward is designed
  to run alongside Microsoft Defender, not to replace every other protection. Keep backups.
- **It acts on your files only when you or your settings say so.** Confirmed threats can be moved to
  the encrypted quarantine automatically (you can switch this off); nothing is deleted unless you
  click Delete. Quarantine can always be undone.
- **Some features change your system on purpose**, and each one says so before it does: switching
  startup entries off, ending programs, cleaning files, registering the script shield (needs your
  administrator approval), starting with Windows, and placing hidden decoy files for the
  ransomware shield. Each can be turned off or undone.
- **Updates are signed.** The app only installs updates and rule packs signed with the Hexward
  release key.
